BUSINESS RULE 12
CUSTOMER TYPE CLASSIFICATION: B2B, B2G, AND B2C

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Classify the buyer as B2B, B2G, B2C, or NOT_VERIFIABLE using structured invoice evidence. Identify evidence-quality anomalies that prevent reliable classification.

EVIDENCE HIERARCHY

Use structured evidence in this order:

1. Explicit BuyerCountry
2. Authoritative buyer account or customer identifier
3. Buyer tax or VAT identifier
4. Government-specific identifier, routing, or endpoint evidence
5. Supported source-system customer classification
6. Buyer name cues only as weak supporting evidence

Do not infer customer type from a name alone.

B2B EVIDENCE

Typical reliable evidence may include:

- usable business customer identifier
- country-consistent tax or VAT identifier
- legal business name and address

Potential anomalies:

- placeholder identifier such as DUMMY or TEST
- invalid identifier format
- identifier prefix inconsistent with BuyerCountry
- conflicting identifiers

B2G EVIDENCE

A public-sector-looking name is insufficient by itself. Reliable B2G classification should use authoritative government evidence, government identifier, or supported routing or endpoint evidence.

If the name suggests a public authority but authoritative evidence is missing or unusable:

issue_type = INSUFFICIENT_GOVERNMENT_EVIDENCE
classification = NOT_VERIFIABLE
suggested_fix = Source validation required

B2C EVIDENCE

Do not infer B2C merely because a VAT number is absent. Evaluate the complete structured evidence. Existing business identifiers or government evidence can override weak consumer cues.

COUNTRY CONSISTENCY

Use country-specific identifier validation only when the rule or format is explicitly supplied. Do not invent a country format.

A buyer identifier with a country prefix inconsistent with explicit BuyerCountry may create IDENTIFIER_COUNTRY_MISMATCH.

FINDING GRANULARITY

Return separate findings for separate evidence problems, including:

- placeholder buyer identifier
- invalid VAT format
- identifier-country mismatch
- insufficient government evidence
- conflicting buyer identifiers

Do not combine all evidence problems into one generic customer-type finding.

CLASSIFICATION OUTPUT

Return one classification:

- B2B
- B2G
- B2C
- NOT_VERIFIABLE

Do not return multiple classifications.

CANONICAL ISSUE TYPES

- PLACEHOLDER_OR_DUMMY_IDENTIFIER
- INVALID_BUYER_IDENTIFIER_FORMAT
- INVALID_BUYER_VAT_FORMAT
- IDENTIFIER_COUNTRY_MISMATCH
- CONFLICTING_BUYER_IDENTIFIERS
- INSUFFICIENT_GOVERNMENT_EVIDENCE
- INSUFFICIENT_B2B_EVIDENCE
- INSUFFICIENT_B2C_EVIDENCE
- INCOMPLETE_VALIDATION_SCOPE

SHARED EXECUTION CONTRACT

Apply the shared sections defined once in the Business Rule 7 prompt: the UNIVERSAL EXECUTION CONTRACT, the RULE OWNERSHIP MATRIX, the RULE COMPLETENESS MANDATE, the GLOBAL DEDUPLICATION AND ANTI-DUPLICATE-CARD CONTRACT (including UI CARD BINDING and LINE-LEVEL FIELD NAMING), and the COMBINED OUTPUT SCHEMA. Do not restate them here.

BR12 ADDITIONAL OUTPUT FIELD

Include:

"customer_type": "<B2B | B2G | B2C | NOT_VERIFIABLE>"

BR12 FINAL SELF-CHECK

- Structured evidence was preferred over names.
- Name alone did not determine B2G.
- Missing VAT alone did not determine B2C.
- No identifier was invented.
- Country formatting was not guessed.
- Each evidence issue is a separate finding.
- Exactly one classification is returned.

OUTPUT SCHEMA

Use the COMBINED OUTPUT SCHEMA defined once in the Business Rule 7 prompt; emit BR12 results inside the single consolidated object per invoice. Tag every BR12 check and finding with "BusinessRule" = BR12, and include the BR12 additional output field customer_type in the BR12 rule_result.
